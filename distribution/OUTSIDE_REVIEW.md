# Optional outside review

An outside reviewer can give a second opinion on a substantial new note prepared
for the human to read. It is not required. No account, provider, quota or permission
to upload research comes with Aw. Choose and authorize a reviewer separately;
plain copied excerpts and returned comments are sufficient.

Ask for comprehensibility to the stated reader, usable notation, explicit proof
steps and worked calculations where a central inference would otherwise be
missing. Cover exposition as well as proofs. Preserve curated wording and the
original claim's assumptions; a style pass is not permission to strengthen it.

Save feedback as **Outside weak critic — unverified suggestions**, with the exact
source/version and reviewer attribution in the owning notebook. Evaluate it
independently; do not treat it as proof validation, a human preference label or
permission to apply every suggestion. Keep review bounded to relevant new or
substantially revised material, not repeated loops over settled prose.

Skip when access, allowance, connectivity or time makes a useful pass impractical.
Continue your own checks and briefly record that it was skipped. Do not wait for
a reset, buy usage or change providers automatically. Scratch exploration needs
no outside review.
